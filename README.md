# Frutiger: Infinite Aero

A complete browser-based first-person atmospheric exploration game built with Three.js, WebGL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Explore an effectively infinite procedural megastructure inspired by Frutiger Aero, beautiful Backrooms, liminal luxury resorts, airports, aquariums, indoor gardens, skybridges, waterparks, and Vista-era digital utopianism. There are no enemies, weapons, quests, collectibles, fail states, or jump scares: the world itself is the content.

## Features

- First-person mouse-look movement with walking, sprinting, acceleration, head bob, camera sway, and FOV toggle.
- Seeded chunk streaming that loads nearby procedural districts and unloads distant ones.
- Multiple atmospheric districts: indoor gardens, aquariums, corporate oases, skybridges, observation decks, digital sanctuaries, neon waterparks, airports, artificial beaches, crystal tunnels, and Vista realms.
- Procedural architecture, props, signage, plants, water, glass, crystal, lighting, and environmental storytelling details.
- Material library with generated procedural textures, glass, water, chrome, marble, sand, vegetation, and crystal surfaces.
- Bloom, tone mapping, fog, soft shadows, colored lights, haze, and dreamlike Vista-era presentation.
- Browser-native evolving ambient audio layers for HVAC hums, machinery, water-like tones, and district-sensitive filtering.

## Controls

- Click: enter pointer-lock and start audio
- WASD: move
- Shift: sprint
- Mouse: look
- F: toggle wider field of view
